The Legend of Juan

$USD juan started as a joke in a tiny roadside taco stand somewhere between the border of traditional finance and the wild west of crypto. Juan was just a regular guy running "El Mercado" — a beat-up food truck serving the best tacos in town.

Every day, customers complained about inflation, banks freezing transfers, and dollars losing value. Juan didn't understand crypto at first — he only understood: "People trust tacos more than banks."

One night, Juan overheard crypto traders arguing about stablecoins collapsing. He laughed, tipped his sombrero, and said the words that changed everything:

"Why not make a coin backed by vibes, tacos, and the strength of the people?"

The traders thought it was hilarious. By sunrise, $USD juan was born.
