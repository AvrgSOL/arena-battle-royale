The Origin Story

The Legend of Juan

Juan was a regular guy running "El Mercado" — a beat-up food truck that somehow always had a line. One day a customer tried to pay with USDC. Juan had never heard of it. He Googled it. He read about algorithmic stablecoins. He read about UST. He read about $40 billion disappearing overnight.

He closed the laptop, flipped a taco, and thought: "These people need something more stable than that. Something they can believe in."

Then he looked at his register. His tacos had held their value for 11 years. His prices never crashed. His liquidity never got drained. No VC had ever rugged his salsa.

That night, on a greasy napkin between orders, Juan wrote the whitepaper. It was three words:

"Pegged to the dream."

The next morning he deployed on Solana. The lawyers called it a meme coin. Juan called it the most honest dollar on the internet.
